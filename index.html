Business Ethics and Professional Leadership

Ethical Dilemma Warm-Up & Strategic Context

"If an advertising campaign is legally compliant but emotionally manipulative and misleading to consumers, is it ethically acceptable?"

In modern marketing, sales, and corporate leadership, legality is merely the baseline. Business ethics encompasses integrity, fairness, accountability, confidentiality, professional boundaries, and transparent advertising. Leaders must make principled decisions that balance profitability with moral responsibility.

1

Core Principles of Business Ethics

Integrity, fairness, accountability, transparency, confidentiality, and respect for professional boundaries in all corporate interactions.

Confidentiality & Privacy:

Safeguarding proprietary client data and consumer market research with strict ethical boundaries.

Ethical Marketing & Advertising

Analyzing deceptive advertising, exaggerated claims, emotional manipulation of vulnerable consumer groups, and honest customer communication.

Truth in Advertising:

Ensuring promotional claims accurately reflect product capabilities without misleading imagery or hidden fees.

Ethical Issues in Workplace Conduct

Navigating conflicts of interest, bribery, workplace harassment, fair credit distribution, and professional integrity among colleagues.

Accountability Standard:

Accepting full responsibility for strategic errors rather than shifting blame onto junior staff.
